# PUG rules
Effective 1 January 2027

PUGs are pick-up games any linked player can join through the WACS Discord. These rules add to the Code of conduct.

# 1. About PUGs

1.1 A PUG is one map, five against five, with teams picked by two captains.
1.2 Results count towards your points and the season leaderboard. See section 8.
1.3 The fair play rules in Code of conduct section 6 apply in every PUG.
1.4 Staff can cancel or restart a PUG to fix a problem. A cancelled PUG doesn't change anyone's points.
1.5 Each PUG has a match number, shown in the Discord. Give it whenever you contact staff about a PUG.
1.6 Use /link to link your accounts, /stats for your stats, /leaderboard for the season and /help for the other commands.

# 2. Joining

2.1 Link your Steam and Discord accounts at wa-cs.com before you queue. See Code of conduct section 4.1.
2.2 Queue by joining a PUG lobby voice channel in the Discord, and leave the queue by leaving the channel. A PUG starts when a lobby has 10 players.
2.3 You can be in only one lobby or match at a time.
2.4 Only queue when you have time for a full match, about an hour.
2.5 You can't queue while you have a queue ban. You're removed from lobby channels until it ends.
2.6 Queue only on your own linked account, under Code of conduct section 3.1.

# 3. Captains and picks

3.1 Captains are chosen by captain rating, a hidden rating used only to pick captains. It goes up when your team wins and down when it loses, and moves further when the result is unexpected.
3.2 You need 10 rated PUGs to be in the captain pool. The 4 players in the lobby with the highest captain scores form the pool, and 2 of them are drawn at random.
3.3 If fewer than 4 players in the lobby have 10 rated PUGs, the pool is filled with other players from the lobby.
3.4 Captain rating carries across seasons and doesn't reset. It drops slowly while you're not playing.
3.5 Captain ratings are hidden from everyone, including you. [wa-cs.com/announcements/captain-rating](https://wa-cs.com/announcements/captain-rating) explains how the rating works.
3.6 The captain with the lower captain score picks first. The first captain picks one player, then each captain picks two in turn, and the first captain makes the last pick.
3.7 Captains have 3 minutes to finish picking. If they don't, the PUG is cancelled and everyone has to queue again.
3.8 Captains can pick anyone in the lobby. Don't arrange picks or swap teams before or after the picks.

# 4. Maps and sides

4.1 Captains ban maps in turn until one is left. The captain who picked first bans first.
4.2 Maps come from the PUG map pool, which staff set from the Active Duty pool. Staff can leave a map out, for example when an update breaks it.
4.3 Captains have 3 minutes to finish banning. If they don't, the PUG is cancelled and everyone has to queue again.
4.4 A ban can't be undone once it's made.
4.5 Starting sides are decided by a knife round. The winning team has 60 seconds to choose a side.

# 5. Ready check

## 5.1 Readying up
5.1.1 When a lobby fills, everyone has 60 seconds to press Ready in the Discord.
5.1.2 After the veto, join the server within 5 minutes. The match goes live when all 10 players are in.

## 5.2 Missing the ready check
5.2.1 If you don't press Ready in time, you're removed from the lobby and can get a queue ban.
[penalties: missed_ready]
5.2.2 Players who pressed Ready stay in the lobby, and the ready check runs again once it refills.
5.2.3 Staff can clear a missed ready check for an emergency. Ask in a ticket and say what happened.

## 5.3 Not joining the server
5.3.1 If anyone doesn't join the server in time, the match is cancelled and nobody gains or loses points.
5.3.2 A player who doesn't join gets an abandon. See section 7.2.

# 6. Match settings

6.1 Matches are MR12, so the first team to 13 rounds wins.
6.2 A 12–12 tie goes to overtime. Overtime is MR3 with $10,000 start money, repeated until there's a winner.
6.3 Each team has 3 tactical pauses of 30 seconds, plus 1 more in each overtime.
6.4 Players can't call technical pauses. If the server or the game breaks, ask staff for help in the Discord.
6.5 Friendly fire and team collision are on.
6.6 If the server crashes, staff restore the round from its backup, or cancel the match with no points gained or lost.
6.7 Talk only with your own team during a match. Listening to the other team is ghosting under Code of conduct section 6.4.1.
6.8 Don't call votes in the game's vote menu, including to kick, surrender or time out.
6.9 Only the 10 players, staff and spectators added by staff can join a PUG server.
6.10 Play under the name on your WACS profile, or one staff can easily match to it.

# 7. Leaving, throwing and subs

## 7.1 Disconnects
7.1.1 If you disconnect, reconnect as fast as you can. The match doesn't pause or restart for a disconnect.
7.1.2 If you can't get back, staff can bring in a sub.
7.1.3 Staff decide whether a long disconnect is an abandon.

## 7.2 Abandons
7.2.1 Leaving a live match, or not joining the server, without a good reason is an abandon.
[penalties: abandon]
7.2.2 Staff can clear an abandon for an emergency or a server problem.

## 7.3 Throwing
7.3.1 Play to win every round. Throwing, griefing or going AFK on purpose is banned.
[penalties: throwing]
7.3.2 Flashing, damaging or blocking your own teammates on purpose is griefing.
7.3.3 Staff use the GOTV demo and reports to decide whether a player threw.

## 7.4 Subs
7.4.1 Staff can bring in a sub to finish a match. Staff choose a linked player who isn't in another match or serving a queue ban.
7.4.2 A sub gets the match result like everyone else.
7.4.3 A player who is replaced gets a loss, whatever the result.

## 7.5 Reports
7.5.1 Report a PUG problem in a ticket within 24 hours, with the match number.
7.5.2 Staff can change a PUG's result or points after a report under section 8.6.

# 8. Points and seasons

8.1 You gain 3 points for a win and lose 2 for a loss. Points never go below 0.
8.2 Each season has its own leaderboard, ranked by points, then win rate, then Rating.
8.3 Staff announce each season's dates, and any prizes, in the Discord.
8.4 Points reset to 0 when a new season starts.
8.5 The leaderboard also shows each player's Rating from their PUG stats. Rating doesn't change points.
8.6 Staff can correct points after a server fault, a replayed match or a rule breach.
8.7 Arranging PUGs to farm points, or losing on purpose to give someone points, is match fixing under Code of conduct section 6.5.1.
8.8 Staff can remove points gained by smurfing, account sharing or match fixing.
