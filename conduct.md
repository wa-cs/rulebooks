# Code of conduct
Effective 1 January 2027

These rules apply to everyone who plays in, casts or runs a WACS match, and to everyone in the WACS Discord. The PUG rules and Competition rules add to them.

To contact staff, open a ticket at [wa-cs.com/support](https://wa-cs.com/support). Don't DM staff directly.

# 1. About these rules

1.1 There are three rule books. This one applies everywhere. The PUG rules cover PUGs and the Competition rules cover events.
1.2 Playing in a WACS match or joining the WACS Discord means you accept these rules.
1.3 Where two rules differ, the more specific one wins. An event page wins over the Competition rules, and the Competition and PUG rules win over this book.
1.4 Not knowing a rule is no defence.
1.5 Staff can make any ruling needed to keep a match or event fair. That includes ruling on cases these rules don't cover, and departing from a written rule where following it would be unfair.
1.6 A ruling under section 1.5 applies to that case only. It doesn't change the rule.
1.7 Staff rulings stand unless they're changed on appeal under section 9.2.
1.8 Rules can change at any time. Changes are announced in the Discord and apply from the effective date at the top of each book.

# 2. Definitions

- **Staff**: WACS admins and officials who run matches and make rulings.
- **Event**: A league, cup, LAN or draft event run by WACS.
- **Event page**: The event's page on wa-cs.com. It sets the event's format, dates, fee and match settings.
- **Match**: A contest between two teams through WACS, in a PUG or an event. An event match can be played over several maps.
- **Map**: One game on one map within a match.
- **Round**: One round of a map.
- **Live**: A map is live from the first round that counts until its last round ends. Warmup and the knife round aren't live. A round is live from the end of its freeze time until it ends.
- **Linked**: Having your Steam and Discord accounts connected on wa-cs.com.
- **Roster**: The players, subs and coach a team has registered for an event.
- **Captain**: The player who leads a team in an event, or one of the two players who pick teams in a PUG.
- **Core player**: One of the 3 players a league team's captain names at signup.
- **Sub**: A player on a roster who isn't one of the team's starting 5.
- **Coach**: The person on a roster who advises the team but doesn't play.
- **Tactical pause**: A pause a team calls to talk about tactics.
- **Technical pause**: A pause called for a problem with the game, a computer, a connection or the server.
- **Demo**: A recording of a map that can be replayed in the game.
- **GOTV demo**: The demo the server records of every WACS map.
- **POV demo**: A demo recorded from one player's own view on their own computer.
- **Bug**: Anything in the game or a map that doesn't work as designed.
- **Exploit**: Using a bug to gain an advantage.
- **Queue ban**: A period where you can't join PUGs.
- **Ruling**: A decision staff make about a match, a player or a team.

# 3. Accounts

3.1 One person, one account. Play every WACS match on your own linked Steam account.
3.2 Don't share your account, play on someone else's, or let someone play on yours.
3.3 Smurfing is banned. That means playing on a second account, or on any account that hides who you are or how good you are.
[penalties: smurfing]
3.4 Keep your Steam profile public while you're in an event, so staff can check it.
3.5 Names and avatars can't be offensive, and can't advertise gambling, betting or adult sites.
3.6 In an event, play under the name on your WACS profile, or one staff can easily match to it.
3.7 Don't pretend to be another player, a team or staff, in a name, an avatar or anywhere else.
[penalties: impersonation]

# 4. Eligibility

4.1 You must be linked to play PUGs or enter events.
4.2 You must live in Western Australia. Living in WA for work or study counts.
4.3 You must be 16 or older. Under 16, you can play with written consent from a parent or guardian, sent to staff in a ticket.
4.4 Staff can ask for proof of where you live or how old you are.
4.5 An event can set its own residency and age rules on its event page.
4.6 A current cheating ban from Valve, an anti-cheat service or another tournament organiser can make you ineligible, wherever it happened. Tell staff about one if they ask. Staff decide whether it applies.

# 5. Behaviour

## 5.1 Everywhere
5.1.1 Treat players, staff, casters and spectators with respect. Trash talk is allowed as long as it doesn't become abuse.
5.1.2 Excessive toxicity is banned. That means constant flaming, baiting or abuse of teammates or opponents, in voice, chat or names.
[penalties: toxicity]
5.1.3 Harassment is banned. That means targeting someone with repeated abuse or unwanted contact, in a match, in the Discord or anywhere else.
[penalties: harassment]
5.1.4 Hate speech is banned. That includes racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, and abuse of anyone for who they are.
[penalties: hate]
5.1.5 Threats and intimidation are banned, including threats to hack, dox or hurt someone.
[penalties: threats]
5.1.6 Staff can act on behaviour outside WACS, for example on social media or at another event, when it targets WACS players, staff or matches.

## 5.2 In the Discord
5.2.1 Keep channels on topic. No spam, advertising or unsolicited DMs to members.
5.2.2 No sexual, violent or shock content.

## 5.3 Towards staff
5.3.1 Follow staff instructions during a match.
5.3.2 Don't mislead staff. That includes false information in a ticket, a report or an appeal, and edited or made-up evidence.
5.3.3 If you disagree with a ruling, appeal it under section 9.2. Don't argue it out in public channels.

## 5.4 Spectators, casters and staff
5.4.1 These rules apply to spectators and casters too. Staff can remove anyone who breaks them from a server, a channel or a broadcast.
5.4.2 Staff don't rule on a match their own team plays in. Another member of staff does.

# 6. Fair play

## 6.1 Cheating
6.1.1 Cheating is banned. That includes aimbots, wallhacks, radar hacks, triggerbots and any other software that gives you an advantage.
[penalties: cheating]
6.1.2 Hardware that cheats is banned too, including DMA cards, input automation devices and anything that reads or changes the game from outside it.
6.1.3 Modified game files are banned, including changed textures, models, sounds and HUD files.

## 6.2 Bugs and exploits
6.2.1 Play the game and its maps as they're designed. Exploiting a bug is banned.
[penalties: exploit]
6.2.2 These are banned:
- pixel walking, which is standing or sitting on an invisible edge where the map has no visible surface
- boosting onto an invisible edge
- seeing, shooting or throwing through a surface that should block it
- leaving the map's playable area, or getting inside walls, floors or props
- planting the bomb where it can't be seen, or can't be reached to defuse it
- planting or defusing the bomb through a wall or floor
- any bug that hides a player's model or sound
6.2.3 These are allowed:
- boosting onto any surface the map lets players stand on
- defusing while opponents are still alive nearby
- movement the game allows without help from a bind, such as bunny hopping
- throwing grenades over walls and roofs that let them through
6.2.4 Staff can ban more positions or bugs before or during an event. Bans are announced in the Discord and apply from then.
6.2.5 If you find a bug during a match, don't use it, and tell staff after the match. Staff decide whether a bug was used on purpose.

## 6.3 Inputs and configs
6.3.1 Use only the game's own settings, commands and config files.
6.3.2 Scripts and macros are banned. That includes any bind, program or device that makes more than one movement or attack action from one key press, such as jump-throw and null binds.
6.3.3 Snap-tap and other SOCD features, which decide between opposite movement keys for you, are banned. Rapid trigger and adjustable actuation are allowed.
6.3.4 Graphics driver settings such as colour, vibrance, brightness and scaling are allowed.
6.3.5 Overlays that show anything about the match beyond what the game shows are banned. Voice and chat overlays are allowed.
6.3.6 A banned input, config or file is penalised as an exploit under section 6.2.1.

## 6.4 Outside information
6.4.1 Ghosting and stream sniping are banned. That means getting information about your match from a stream, a spectator or anyone outside it while you play.
[penalties: sniping]
6.4.2 During a live round, only the players in the match can talk to their team. The Competition rules say when a coach can.

## 6.5 Match fixing and betting
6.5.1 Match fixing is banned. That means agreeing a result, losing on purpose, or passing on information to help someone bet.
[penalties: fixing]
6.5.2 Don't bet on a WACS match you play in, coach, cast or run, and don't help anyone else bet on one.

## 6.6 Reviews
6.6.1 Staff can review any match, and can remove a player or hold a result while they look into it.

# 7. Evidence and demos

7.1 Every WACS map is recorded as a GOTV demo. Staff use GOTV demos to rule on reports, protests and appeals.
7.2 GOTV demos are kept for at least 90 days.
7.3 Staff can also use clips, screenshots, chat logs, voice recordings and anything else they find reliable.
7.4 Staff can ask a player for their POV demo or a screenshot of their game settings. At a LAN, staff can also check a player's computer and peripherals.
7.5 Send what staff ask for within 24 hours. Refusing, or not sending it in time without a good reason, counts against you, and staff rule on the evidence they have.

# 8. Penalties

8.1 Staff can give warnings, queue bans, event bans, forfeits, disqualification from an event, loss of a prize, and Discord timeouts or bans.
8.2 Each offence has its own penalty steps, shown under its rule. A repeat offence moves you to the next step while earlier offences still count.
8.3 Staff can skip steps or go straight to a ban for a serious offence, and can reduce a penalty for an emergency.
8.4 A ban applies to the person. Playing on another account while banned is smurfing under section 3.3.
8.5 A team answers for what its players and coach do in its matches. Staff can award a map or the match to the other team for a breach.

# 9. Reports and appeals

## 9.1 Reports
9.1.1 Report a player or a problem by opening a ticket. Say which match it was and what happened.
9.1.2 Report a match within 24 hours of it ending. Report anything else as soon as you can.
9.1.3 Include clips or screenshots if you have them. Staff can ask for more under section 7.4.
9.1.4 To protest the result of an event match, follow Competition rules section 10.

## 9.2 Appeals
9.2.1 Appeal a ruling or a penalty within 72 hours by opening a ticket.
9.2.2 Say why the ruling is wrong or too harsh, and include anything staff didn't have before.
9.2.3 A member of staff who wasn't involved in the ruling reviews it. Their decision is final.
9.2.4 A penalty stays in place while it's being appealed.

# 10. Media

10.1 WACS can stream, record, clip and publish any WACS match, and use player and team names and logos with it.
10.2 You can stream your own PUGs. Streaming your own event matches needs approval under Competition rules section 12.2.
